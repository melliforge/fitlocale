# Refund policy

_Last updated 2026-10-22_

- **14 days, no questions:** if fitlocale cloud isn't right for you, ask for a full refund within 14 days of your first payment.
- **How to ask:** Paddle, our reseller, handles payments and refunds. Find your order at [paddle.net](https://paddle.net) with the email you paid with, and ask for a refund there.
- **Renewals:** cancel before your plan renews to avoid the next charge. You can cancel from your account page at [cloud.fitlocale.dev/account](https://cloud.fitlocale.dev/account). A renewal charged by mistake, for example after a cancellation that didn't go through, is refunded in full on request.
- **Switching plans:** when you change between Team and Business, Paddle charges or credits the difference for the rest of the period right away.
- **Trial:** the 14-day trial needs no card and costs nothing.
