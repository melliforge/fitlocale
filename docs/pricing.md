# Pricing

fitlocale is free and open source, and stays complete: every check, the report, the baseline, the CLI and the [GitHub Action](/reference/github-action), in [any CI](/reference/other-ci).

**fitlocale cloud** is the paid part. It brings the findings to your pull requests: one comment with a cropped screenshot of each new problem, and the full report hosted behind GitHub sign-in.

| | Team | Business |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $49/month or $490/year | $149/month or $1,490/year |
| Repositories | 3 | Unlimited |
| Reports kept for | 30 days | 90 days |

Installing the [fitlocale GitHub App](https://github.com/apps/fitlocale-cloud/installations/new) starts a 14-day trial, with no card. The full details, terms and refund policy are at [cloud.fitlocale.dev/pricing](https://cloud.fitlocale.dev/pricing). To send reports to it, add `upload: true` to the [Action](/reference/github-action#upload).
